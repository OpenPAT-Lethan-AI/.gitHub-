openPAT known as (PheslaX Alpha Technologies)

openPAT is a research, development, and deployment company focused on artificial intelligence, software, and the technologies built around them.

Lethan AI

Lethan is openPAT's artificial intelligence platform, bringing together conversation, research, tools, models, multi-agent collaboration, scheduling, and interactive AI experiences.

Perspective

Perspective is Lethan's interactive AI experience powered by ALM (Action Language Model).

Instead of being limited to text responses, Perspective allows the AI to interact with a digital workspace through actions.

Inside its workspace, the AI can:

- Open and use a board
- Write information
- Draw diagrams
- Organize information visually
- Explain concepts through the workspace
- Coordinate its actions with its explanation

Perspective uses Humanoid-LMT 2, an Action Language Model developed for action-based AI interaction.

TheTeam

TheTeam is Lethan's multi-agent system.

Multiple AI agents can meet, communicate, research, exchange information, and work together on a shared task.

Technologies

LMT

LMT — Lethan Model Technology is a core technology across the Lethan ecosystem and the technology parent of ALM.

ALM

ALM — Action Language Model is designed for AI interaction through actions within digital environments rather than text alone.

LCPE

LCPE — Lethan Construction Playground Engine is a behavioral construction and feedback system developed for Lethan.

It connects verified behavioral feedback with reusable constructions that can be stored and retrieved during AI interactions.

Models

openPAT works with and develops models across its technologies and products.

Current model families include:

- Humanoid-LMT — Action Language Model family
- Orbital-LMT — Large Language Model family

Research

openPAT conducts research and development across AI models, action-based AI, multi-agent systems, behavioral systems, and other technologies.
